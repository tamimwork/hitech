Healthcare Excellence

Mahbubur Rahman Memorial Hospital

A trusted name in accessible and compassionate healthcare, Mahbubur Rahman Memorial Hospital was established by Rahman Brothers in memory of their father, with a vision to bring quality medical care closer to underserved rural communities. Operating on a non-profit model, the hospital is committed to delivering urban-standard, technology-driven medical treatments at nominal costs.

The facility is equipped with state-of-the-art German-engineered Siemens CT Scan technology, advanced laparoscopic and endoscopy units, modern clinical diagnostic laboratories, and round-the-clock emergency ambulance services.

Youth Empowerment & Education

Nursing Institute & Career Development

Established under Rahman Group's social welfare initiative, the Nursing Institute provides high-quality diploma education to meritorious students from underprivileged rural backgrounds.

Our graduates consistently demonstrate exceptional professional standards and are currently serving across various government hospitals and medical institutions nationwide, driving socio-economic upliftment for their families.
