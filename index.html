Our History

Since the 1980s, the Rahman brothers have grown from the iron and steel business to the garments industry with Rahman Knit Garments ltd, and later strengthened their legacy by establishing Hi-Tech steel & re-rolling mills ltd.

Our Mission

To provide its customers with the best possible satisfaction and respect for their investment by facilitating them with strong and resilient infrastructure solution.

Our Vision

To emerge as a premier manufacturer of steel that will provide the nation a strong and reliable infrastructure.

Manufacturing

From molten metal to monumental strength

Induction Melting Furnace

Hi-Tech has an Induction Melting Furnace with a capacity of 8 Metric ton. This high capacity unit is manufactured by Megatherm Furnaces Pvt. Ltd., India. Scraps are melted into liquid steel here. The electromagnetic coil heats the charge to roughly 1,600°C, and once fully molten, the melt is sampled and sent for spectrometric analysis so carbon, manganese and silicon levels can be corrected before tapping.

  • Furnace Capacity8 Metric Ton
  • ManufacturerMegatherm Furnaces Pvt. Ltd., India
  • Melting Temperature~1,600°C
  • OutputMolten Steel / Cast Billets
8 MT Capacity

Continuous Casting Machine (CCM)

Our plant is equipped with a modern Continuous Casting Machine (CCM) that transforms molten steel into high-quality billets with exceptional dimensional accuracy and consistency. The automated casting process ensures continuous production, minimizes material loss, reduces energy consumption, and improves overall manufacturing efficiency. By maintaining precise cooling and solidification control, the CCM produces billets with superior surface quality and internal structure, providing the ideal raw material for the rolling mill and ensuring reliable performance in every stage of steel production.

  • Machine TypeContinuous Casting Machine (CCM)
  • FunctionConverts Molten Steel into Billets
  • Production ProcessContinuous Automated Casting
  • Key BenefitsHigh Precision, Reduced Waste & Energy Efficiency
  • Final OutputHigh-Quality Steel Billets
Continuous Casting

Re-heating Furnace

Natural gas fired re-heating furnace equipped with motor driven mechanical pusher. Steel Bars are heated in rolling temperature (1100 to 1200 degrees Celsius). Standard capacity is 10MT per hour. Billets travel through clearly defined heating zones so the core and surface reach a uniform temperature together, keeping the steel soft and consistent enough to roll without cracking downstream. The furnace also incorporates hot charging technology, allowing hot billets to be charged directly, improving energy efficiency and reducing overall processing time.

  • Fuel TypeNatural Gas
  • MechanismMotor Driven Mechanical Pusher
  • Rolling Temperature1100°C – 1200°C
  • Standard Capacity10 MT per hour
  • TechnologyHot Charging Technology
10 MT / hour

Roughing Mill and Intermediate Mill

The roughing mill includes Y-Table, proper guide box, twist pipe and auto feed repeaters. 1000 HP induction motor runs the roughing mill and intermediate mill, adjoined by a dynamically balanced 8 MT fly wheel. The heated billet passes through a series of grooved rolls that progressively reduce its cross-section and stretch it into a long strand, with guide boxes and twist pipes keeping it correctly aligned between every stand.

  • Motor Power1000 HP Induction Motor
  • Fly Wheel8 MT, Dynamically Balanced
  • Key ComponentsY-Table, Guide Box, Twist Pipe
  • Feed SystemAuto Feed Repeaters
1000 HP Motor

Finishing Mill and Auto-cooling Bed

10-inch Finishing Mill has a Continuous Bearing Mill of 2 stands driven by 400HP Slipring Induction Motor, capable of producing about 10 MT deformed bars per hour. The auto cooling bed handles hot bars in high speed operation and allows bars to cool in ambient temperature. This is the stage where the bar's final diameter and its deformed rib pattern are set, and controlled cooling afterward governs the steel's final grain structure and strength.

  • Mill Size10-inch Finishing Mill
  • Stands2 Continuous Bearing Mill Stands
  • Motor Power400 HP Slipring Induction Motor
  • Output Capacity~10 MT Deformed Bars / hour
10 MT / hour

Cold Shearing and Dispatch

High performance Cold Shearing Machine cuts bars to desired length. 50MT computerized Weigh Bridge ensures accuracy in weight. Delivery is made within 24 hours after order confirmation. Cooled bars are bundled, tagged with batch and heat-number details for traceability, and cross-checked on the weigh bridge before loading so every shipment matches the order sheet exactly. Every delivery is accompanied by a dispatch card for easy reference and record-keeping.

  • Cutting MachineHigh Performance Cold Shearing Machine
  • Weighing System50 MT Computerized Weigh Bridge
  • Delivery TimeWithin 24 Hours
  • TraceabilityBatch & Heat-Number Tagged
  • Dispatch CardProvided with Every Delivery

Sustainability

Turning Air Pollution into Global Exports

At Hi-Tech Steel & Re-Rolling Mills, we protect local air quality by operating a state-of-the-art Industrial Baghouse Filtration System. This advanced equipment traps fine carbon dust and particulate emissions directly from our furnaces before they can pollute the surrounding environment. Instead of generating local waste, we capture and process these high-purity carbon byproducts into valuable raw materials.

We then export this collected carbon internationally — driving a sustainable circular economy while keeping Bangladesh’s air clean.
